# UI brief — 10 Brutal Overclaim Native Landing Pages (Tani Niaga)

## Niche
Direct-response agricultural commerce and crop recovery inputs in Indonesia (horticulture, rice, corn, and shallots).

## Audience
Commercial and smallholder Indonesian farmers (padi, cabai rawit, cabai merah besar, bawang merah, jagung, tomat, kentang) facing acute rainy season threats (patek, busuk umbi kempes, rontok bunga, layu fusarium, rebah badai, asem-aseman). They trust concrete field proof: demplot results, local Poktan names, real before/after imagery, and risk-free cash on delivery (COD).

## Primary job
Convert cold mobile Meta Ads traffic into verified COD orders for single-product high-efficacy agro solutions (Saratoga, Kojien, Bensu) via a fast, zero-friction 480px direct-response landing page with embedded hybrid checkout.

## Primary action
Place an order via the embedded hybrid checkout form (`#checkout-form`) selecting preferred package with COD payment.

1. https://taniniaga.shop/benteng-anti-patek (Best-in-class internal Saratoga fruit defense)

## Non-goals
- Multi-product bundling confusion (strict 1 Product - 1 LP - 1 Angle discipline).
- Artificial corporate AI slop or generic stock photography.
- Complex multi-step checkout modals that increase mobile drop-off.
